About

Paulina Kuo is a physicist at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Her research interests are in quantum networking, integrated photonics and quantum metrology.

Quantum songs and quantum nursery rhymes came about as part of the CLEO Science Slam, where the challenge is to present and explain elements of your research in an accessible, engaging and fun way. When faced with this challenge, the quantum nursery rhymes were born.
